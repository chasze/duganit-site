About

I'm Chase Dugan. Over six years I've worked my way from the help desk through IT management and into engineering. Today I'm a systems and network engineer at a managed service provider, where I look after small and mid-sized businesses: their networks, their Microsoft 365 tenants, and the backups everything else depends on.

A normal week has me working across more than 50 Microsoft 365 and Entra ID tenants, UniFi, SonicWall, FortiGate and Cisco gear, and Veeam backup infrastructure. I've moved dozens of sites from SonicWall to UniFi by rebuilding firewall configs by hand, led several 3CX to Teams Phone migrations, and I'm usually the one who writes the documentation once a hard problem is solved.

This site is where that documentation habit goes public. You'll find my homelab builds, my Azure study notes, and write-ups of problems worth remembering.
